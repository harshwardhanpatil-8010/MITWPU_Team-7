# ParkyCare

ParkyCare is a lifestyle and wellness app designed to support individuals living with Parkinson’s disease through guided exercise, rhythmic movement training, medication management, symptom tracking, and therapeutic cognitive activities.

While Parkinson’s disease currently has no cure, research has shown that consistent physical activity, cognitive therapy, and structured routines can help slow the progression of symptoms and improve overall quality of life.

ParkyCare focuses on helping users stay active, independent, and engaged through structured daily activities that support both physical and cognitive well-being.

---

## Features

### Guided Exercise

ParkyCare includes specially designed exercise sessions aimed at improving mobility, coordination, flexibility, and overall daily movement.

#### 10-Minute Workouts

Personalized guided workout routines powered by a specialized algorithm that considers:
- The patient’s stage of Parkinson’s disease
- Whether medication has been taken

Based on these factors, the app curates customized workout modules tailored to the user’s condition and daily readiness, helping make exercise safer, more effective, and more accessible.

#### Rhythmic Walking

A beat-based walking exercise designed to:
- Improve walking stability
- Enhance stride rhythm
- Help reduce gait disturbances commonly associated with Parkinson’s disease

---

### Therapy Games

Interactive therapeutic games designed to support cognitive health by improving:
- Memory
- Focus
- Reaction time
- Coordination
- Mental responsiveness

These activities are built to be simple, engaging, and enjoyable while encouraging regular cognitive stimulation.

---

### Medication Management

ParkyCare helps users stay consistent with their treatment through a built-in medication management and reminder system.

#### Medication Reminders

Timely reminders help ensure that users do not miss important medications throughout the day, supporting:
- Better routine management
- Improved treatment consistency
- Daily medication adherence

---

### Symptom Tracking

ParkyCare includes a symptom tracking system where users can record and monitor symptom intensity over time.

This feature helps users:
- Better understand their condition
- Identify symptom patterns
- Maintain a consistent health record
- Support discussions during medical consultations

---

## Our Goal

ParkyCare is built with the goal of improving the everyday lives of people living with Parkinson’s disease by promoting:
- Consistency
- Movement
- Therapy
- Independence

Through an easy-to-use digital experience, the app aims to empower users to take an active role in their wellness journey while supporting both their physical and cognitive health.
